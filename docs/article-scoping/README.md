# Article Scoping: Response Field Mapping Paper

Research analysis scoping the scientific article on response field mapping
of human low-threshold mechanoreceptors during naturalistic social touch.

Dataset: 12 sessions, 5 subjects (ST13–ST18), 9 analyzed Aβ
(10 including the ST15-01 outlier) + 2 CT afferents,
dorsal forearm (hairy skin), naturalistic hand stroking and tapping.

## Key Conclusions

- Response fields (288–1364 mm²) are ~17–210× larger than classical probe RFs — expected and explained by naturalistic vs. punctate stimulus
- 5 high-novelty contributions identified, led by the inflection-contour boundary method and SLIM UV parameterization
- Closest competitor (Gerling 2025) is complementary, not competing — they report discrimination, we report spatial organization
- 2D feature grids not feasible (too few touches); 1D velocity grid works
- n=2 per subtype limits inferential statistics; frame as descriptive characterization
