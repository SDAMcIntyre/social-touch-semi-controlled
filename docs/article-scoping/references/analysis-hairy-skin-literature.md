## Literature Landscape — What Exists and What Does Not

### Well-characterised

- **CT velocity tuning**: inverted-U peaking at 1-3 cm/s, correlated with
  pleasantness (Loken 2009, Ackerley 2014, Vallbo 1999)
- **CT thermal tuning**: optimal at neutral skin temperature 32 deg C
  (Ackerley et al. 2014)
- **Myelinated types on forearm**: five types with RF sizes from scanning
  probe (Vallbo et al. 1995)
- **CT RF structure**: multi-hotspot, force-dependent area expansion
  (Wessberg et al. 2003)
- **CT central projection**: insular cortex, not S1 — limbic/affective
  pathway (Olausson et al. 2002, Morrison 2016)

### Not characterised — gaps the naturalistic study addresses

1. **No RF mapping with naturalistic (whole-hand) stimuli on hairy skin.**
   Every existing study used point probes (von Frey, monofilaments, scanning
   probes) or controlled robotic brushes. The response field under full-hand
   social touch has never been mapped.

2. **No spatial characterisation of gesture-specific response fields.**
   Xu et al. 2025 showed that SA-II and HFA can temporally discriminate
   social gestures, but did not map the spatial footprint of each gesture.

3. **No UV-parameterised contact area measurement on forearm skin.**
   Hauser & Gerling 2019 tracked hand position with Leap Motion but lacked
   mesh-based contact area and depth. The Kinect + SLIM UV approach is novel.

4. **No population-level response field model for hairy skin.**
   Johansson & Vallbo 1980 built a tiling/sheath model for glabrous hand;
   no equivalent exists for forearm skin.
