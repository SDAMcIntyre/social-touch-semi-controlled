# Classification — Receptive Field (Extended Literature)

Extended survey of human receptive field papers beyond the core set already
catalogued in `classification-receptive-field.md`. Compiled via systematic
web search (2026-06-08). Entries are grouped by skin site and ordered
chronologically within each group.

Papers already present in the core classification file are **not repeated
here** — see `classification-receptive-field.md` for:

- Vallbo, Olausson, Wessberg, Kakuda 1995 (hairy forearm, 5 Ab types)
- Wessberg, Olausson, Fernstrom, Vallbo 2003 (hairy forearm, CT hotspots)
- Johansson & Vallbo 1979 (glabrous hand, density mapping)
- Johansson & Vallbo 1980 (glabrous hand, RF sizes + tiling model)
- Johansson & Vallbo 1983 (glabrous hand, 4-type review)
- Abraira & Ginty 2013 (multi-species LTMR review)

## Relevance to This Project

### Directly comparable (hairy forearm, peripheral afferents)

The papers most relevant to the naturalistic RF mapping pipeline are:

1. **Vallbo et al. 1993** — CT discovery on forearm (historical anchor)
2. **Edin & Abbs 1991** + **Edin 1992** + **Edin 2004** — myelinated types on hairy dorsal hand, including SA-III class
3. **Edin 2001** — population census on thigh hairy skin (60 units, 7 types)
4. **Loken et al. 2022** — CT properties across body sites including forearm
5. **Moore et al. 2025** — CT-hair follicle coupling (most recent)
6. **Nagi et al. 2019** — A-HTMR discovery (new afferent class on forearm)
7. **Mancini et al. 2014** — forearm 2PD threshold (~27 mm) benchmarks spatial resolution

### Methodologically informative

6. **Pruszynski & Johansson 2014** + **Jarocka et al. 2021** — sub-RF hotspot
   structure mirrors the multi-hotspot patterns the heatmap pipeline captures
7. **Corniani & Saal 2020** — whole-body density estimates needed to
   contextualise RF size measurements against population expectations
8. **Watkins et al. 2022** — SA-II microstimulation demonstrates that SA-II
   contributes to conscious touch perception

### Taxonomy and framing

9. **Handler & Ginty 2021** — current LTMR taxonomy (updates Abraira & Ginty 2013)
10. **Vallbo 2018** — detailed scanning probe method description (historical context for comparing naturalistic vs classical RF mapping)
